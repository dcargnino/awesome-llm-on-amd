# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project aims to be a respectful, technically honest, and welcoming place for people sharing AMD LLM knowledge.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Share evidence when making technical claims.
- Distinguish facts from guesses.
- Help newcomers without gatekeeping.
- Accept correction gracefully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Dismissive or hostile responses
- Knowingly posting misleading technical information
- Spam, self-promotion without relevance, or repetitive low-value submissions

## Reporting

If a discussion becomes disrespectful or unsafe, contact the repository maintainers through the project's issue tracker or another maintainer-provided channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that do not meet these standards.
